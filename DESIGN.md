# Design System: SCOPE CodeStats

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Scoreboard"**

CodeStats is where a student goes to learn how they are doing. The interface behaves like a well-kept scoreboard: numerals large enough to read across a desk, soft surfaces behind them, and a single electric blue that appears only where something can be done or something is selected. The score, the rank and the consistency level are the headline of every screen; navigation, filters and charts stay in support.

Surfaces are soft and generously rounded and are layered by tone, not by shadow. Controls are pills and circles. On every screen exactly one tile is inverted (near-white on Midnight, near-black on Daylight) so the eye lands on the number that matters, and at most one tile is filled with the accent. This is the whole vocabulary. Dense staff screens use the same pieces with tighter spacing and smaller controls; they are not a different style.

Midnight (dark) and Daylight (light) are designed together from the same roles. Neither is derived from the other, and neither is the default by taste: the first visit follows the operating system, and a manual choice is remembered.

The previous look is retired in full: the pure black void, glass blur and glowing edges, 4px buttons, the five competing typefaces, and the `#0088cc` link blue.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Numerals lead. Display numerals are bold, tightly tracked and tabular wherever they are compared.
- Tone, not shadow, carries depth. Cards are flat; only floating layers cast a shadow.
- Pills and circles for every control, 24px corners for every card.
- One electric blue, used for action, selection, focus and links, plus one accent tile at most.
- One spotlight tile per screen, inverted against the theme.
- Status is a soft tint plus an icon plus a word, never colour alone.
- Charts use rounded bars, a muted default and one highlighted bar, with a dark tooltip.
- One typeface, Plus Jakarta Sans, in every UI role.

## Colors

The palette is Restrained with two earned exceptions. Neutrals carry almost every surface (Midnight is a near-black, neutral scale with a faint cool tint; Daylight leans blue-violet, near 235 to 245 degrees); the accent is spent on action and state; the spotlight tile and the single accent tile are the only large fills. Nothing is pure black or pure white as a surface or as body text.

### Named Rules

**The One Voice Rule.** The accent fill covers no more than 15% of any screen, including the accent tile. Its rarity is what makes an action findable.

**The Spotlight Rule.** One inverted tile per screen: the figure the student came for. Two spotlights is none. The active nav pill and the highlighted bar borrow the same colour because they mean "this one".

**The Tint Rule.** Daylight neutrals lean toward the blue-violet hue; Midnight neutrals are near-black with only a faint cool tint. Neither reaches pure black (`#000000`) or pure white. Secondary text on a coloured tile is a tint of that tile's text colour, not grey.

**The Theme Parity Rule.** Every role exists in both themes and every screen is checked in both. A component that only works in one theme is unfinished.

**The Not-Colour-Alone Rule.** Rank change, score change and status always carry an arrow, icon or word in addition to colour.

### Theme behaviour

- First visit follows `prefers-color-scheme`; the choice made with the toggle is stored and wins from then on. Dark is the fallback when the system gives no preference.
- Switching themes is instant. No global `transition` on every element: transition only `background-color`, `color` and `border-color` on the controls that need it, 150ms.
- Images, logos and illustrations ship in both variants or use `currentColor`. Photographs and avatars are never tinted.
- Code editors use matching themes (`scope-midnight`, `scope-daylight`) built from the same roles.
- The resume preview, PDF exports and printed pages are always Daylight, because they represent paper.
- Set `color-scheme` on the root so scrollbars, form controls and the caret follow the theme. Selection uses accent-soft with the theme's text colour.

## Typography

**Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (variable, weights 200 to 800) with a system fallback
**Code font:** the system monospace stack (`ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Cascadia Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace); no download

**Character:** A friendly geometric sans with open, round shapes. At weight 700 to 800 and tight tracking it carries large numerals with confidence; at 400 to 600 it stays calm in tables and forms. It replaces Nekst, Inter, Instrument Serif, Outfit, Sora and Clash Display in the interface. The owner pinned it.

### Hierarchy

Product screens use a fixed rem scale, not fluid sizes, with a tight ratio of about 1.125 to 1.2. Numerals sit on their own scale.

- **Display** (700, 2.25rem, 1.22, -0.02em): The single page heading of a dashboard-style screen, once per page ("Your weekly overview"). Steps down to 1.75rem below 900px.
- **Headline** (700, 1.5rem, 1.33): Page titles on ordinary screens and dialog titles.
- **Title** (600, 1.125rem, 1.45): Card and section titles.
- **Title small** (600, 1rem, 1.5): Card titles inside dense layouts, list item names.
- **Body large** (400, 1rem, 1.625): Reading text, form help on auth screens. Measure 65 to 75ch.
- **Body** (400, 0.875rem, 1.57): Default UI text.
- **Body small** (400, 0.8125rem, 1.54): Table cells, secondary lines.
- **Label** (600, 0.875rem, 1.43): Buttons, tabs, field labels, nav items.
- **Caption** (500, 0.75rem, 1.33, +0.01em): Axis labels, badges, timestamps. 12px is the floor.
- **Numeral XL** (800, 3.5rem, 1.07, -0.03em): The one spotlight figure on a screen.
- **Numeral L** (700, 2.5rem, 1.1, -0.03em): Primary figures on KPI tiles.
- **Numeral M** (700, 1.75rem, 1.14, -0.02em): Secondary figures, table summaries.

### Named Rules

**The One Family Rule.** Plus Jakarta Sans is the only interface face. No serif or display face appears in labels, buttons, data or headings. The resume builder's own font picker (Product Sans and others) is for the user's document only and never styles the interface.

**The Tabular Rule.** Numbers that are compared vertically (tables, leaderboards, ranks, lists of scores) use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, which Plus Jakarta Sans supports. A single standalone hero numeral may stay proportional.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings, buttons and column headers are sentence case. No all-caps text and no tracked-out label above a heading.

**The Floor Rule.** Tracking never goes tighter than -0.04em, nothing is set below 12px, and a display figure is never larger than 6rem.

**The Self-Host Rule.** Ship the font from the app, not from a third-party CDN: `@fontsource-variable/plus-jakarta-sans`, Latin subset first, `font-display: swap`, preload the primary file. The family name registered by that package is `Plus Jakarta Sans Variable`.

## Layout

A 12-column fluid grid inside a shell. The shell is a left rail plus a top bar; content sits in a canvas with generous, consistent gaps. Responsive change is structural (the rail collapses, columns stack), never fluid type.

- **Shell:** left rail 264px expanded, 80px collapsed, collapsed by default below 1200px and a drawer below 900px. Top bar 72px: page search, theme toggle, notifications, account. Public and marketing pages use a top pill nav and no rail.
- **Content width:** fluid up to 1440px, centred. Reading content 72ch. Forms 480 to 560px. Test-taking column 760 to 880px. Code editors run edge to edge.
- **Page padding:** 32px at 1200px and above, 24px at 900 to 1199px, 16px below 900px.
- **Grid gap:** 20px, 16px below 900px, 12px below 600px.
- **Card padding:** 24px, 16px below 600px.
- **Spacing scale:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56. Related things sit 8 to 12px apart, sections 32 to 40px apart. More space above a heading than below it.
- **Density modes:** Comfortable (students) uses 44px controls and 56px table rows. Compact (staff tables and analytics) uses 36px controls and 44px rows. The mode changes sizes only, never colours or shapes.
- **Breakpoints (Material UI defaults):** xs 0, sm 600, md 900, lg 1200, xl 1536.
- **Dashboard composition:** a bento of 12-column spans. The spotlight tile and the primary chart share the first row (for example 8 and 4). Cards in a row share one height. Never a row of identical icon-heading-text cards.
- **Touch:** every target is at least 44px on touch layouts; icon buttons are circles of 44px.
- **Overlays:** menus, popovers and tooltips render in a portal so no ancestor can clip them.
- **Z-order:** content 0, sticky headers 10, rail 20, menus and popovers 30, dialogs 40, toasts 50, tooltips 60.
- **The admin top:** every admin screen opens with the same fixed block, the `AdminTop` component (`src/components/ui/AdminTop.jsx`). Row one is the heading: the title with its count beside it ("13 cohorts"), a tagline under it that is always a single line (about 60 characters or fewer, one plain sentence, cut with an ellipsis if the room is short), and the primary create button at the right end of the row, level with the title. The button and the search row are always there, also when the list is empty or failed to load; the empty state never repeats the button. Row two is one line of controls with the space between them: the search pill at the left (420px at most) and the filter dropdowns, or the tabs, at the right end. A screen with no search (monitoring) puts them at the left. The block rests exactly where it sticks (a negative top margin cancels the page's top padding), so it does not shift when scrolling starts. It is `position: sticky` right under the navbar on the canvas fill, reaches the page's side padding, and shows a 1px `line` hairline under it once the content has scrolled beneath it. Below 600px the tagline is hidden, the search takes a line and the dropdowns share the next one (tabs take it whole and scroll sideways). A screen opened from a list shows its path above the title instead of a back button (see Breadcrumbs). Screens never rebuild this block with their own markup or CSS. It needs `<main>` to be the scroller, so a route using it is listed in `isFixedTopPage` and `isNoFooterPage` in `App.jsx`.
- **Admin list content:** under the admin top, a list of things is a grid of cards (1 column below 600px, 2 from 600px, 3 from 1000px, 4 from 1360px), all cards in a row the same height. A card has a status badge at the top left, the three-dot menu at the top right (Edit, Delete and the like, the destructive item last and in the danger tone), the title, up to two lines of description, the figure that matters in a numeral style, a few one-line facts with small icons, and a hairline above a foot with the "Manage" button at the right. "Manage" is a link to the item's own page, which holds everything about the item; the card never holds the editing itself. In code it is the kit's `AdminListCard` (with `AdminCardGrid`); user groups and mentorships both use it.

### Named Rules

**The Number-First Rule.** The first thing in reading order on a data screen is the figure the student came for, set in a numeral style. Filters and navigation come after it, visually quieter.

**The Same Gap Rule.** One gap value per breakpoint between all cards of a page. Uneven gutters are the fastest way to make a dashboard look unfinished.

**The Same Path Rule.** A screen reached from a list shows where it is as a path (`/ User groups / Group name`) in the admin top, never as a back arrow or a "back to" button.

**The Same Top Rule.** Every admin screen starts with `AdminTop`: title and tagline at the left, the primary action at the right, then the search at the left and the dropdowns at the right of one line. Moving between admin screens never moves the heading, the create button or the search.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal. On Midnight a surface is a step lighter than the canvas and a tile inside it is a step lighter again. On Daylight cards are near-white on a light canvas and tiles inside are a step darker. Cards have no shadow and no border at rest. Only layers that float above the page cast a shadow, and the shadow always has an offset and a soft blur.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Float small** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(8, 10, 24, 0.28)` Midnight; `0 8px 24px rgba(17, 19, 28, 0.10)` Daylight): menus, popovers, date picker.
- **Float medium** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(8, 10, 24, 0.45)` Midnight; `0 16px 40px rgba(17, 19, 28, 0.14)` Daylight): dialogs, the bulk action bar, toasts.
- **Focus ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px <surface>, 0 0 0 4px <focus colour>`): every focusable element, keyboard only (`:focus-visible`).
- **Scrim** (`rgba(10, 12, 24, 0.6)` Midnight; `rgba(17, 19, 28, 0.4)` Daylight): behind dialogs. A flat colour, no blur.

### Named Rules

**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest. A shadow means "this floats over the page" and nothing else.

**The No-Glass Rule.** No backdrop blur, no translucent glass panels, no glowing edges, no inner highlights. The retired `getLiquidGlassStyle` and `getInnerGlassStyle` helpers and every `backdropFilter` in the interface go.

## Shapes

Soft and consistent: one radius family, and the same radius for the same job everywhere.

| Radius | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| xs | 6px | Checkboxes, small marks, code spans |
| sm | 10px | Tooltips, menu items, bars (top corners 8px) |
| md | 14px | Text inputs, text areas, toasts |
| lg | 18px | Tiles inside a card, thumbnails, image frames |
| xl | 24px | Cards and panels |
| 2xl | 28px | Dialogs and sheets |
| pill | 999px | Buttons, chips, badges, tabs, selects, search, progress bars |

- Icon buttons, avatars and calendar days are circles.
- Chart bars have fully rounded top corners (8px) and a flat base.
- Hairlines are 1px `line`. Dividers inside a card are hairlines with 16px of space on each side.
- No coloured left or right border on cards, list items, callouts or alerts. State is a leading icon and a soft tint.
- Icons are Phosphor (`@phosphor-icons/react`): Regular weight at rest and Fill only for the selected item; 20px inline, 24px in navigation, `currentColor`. One icon family only, and no emoji or Unicode glyphs as icons.

## Components

### Buttons

Pills, 44px tall (36px compact, 52px large), label type, 24px horizontal padding.

- **Primary:** accent fill, white label. Hover `#0476C8`, press `#0365AA`. One per view.
- **Secondary:** raised fill, text colour label. Hover one tone up (high).
- **Spotlight:** spotlight fill, on-spotlight label. The strong neutral for the most important non-accent action ("Join class", "Export").
- **Ghost:** no fill, accent-text label, for tertiary actions.
- **Destructive:** danger fill with the theme's matching label colour, always behind a confirmation.
- **Icon button:** 44px circle, raised fill, 20px icon, always has an accessible name and a tooltip.
- **States:** hover, `:focus-visible` ring, pressed, disabled (`high` fill with a `subtle` label, no pointer, no hover, and no opacity change), loading (spinner replaces the icon, width stays fixed, label stays).

### Navigation

- **Rail:** surface fill, grouped items with small sentence-case group headings. Items are 44px pills; the selected item is a spotlight pill with the filled icon. On desktop the rail is icons only until the pointer or the keyboard reaches it, then it opens over the page (the page never reflows) with its width, pills and labels animating for 240 to 320ms. That is the one place layout properties animate, because the rail floats and nothing else is laid out around it.
- **Top pill nav** (public pages and in-page tabs): a pill container (surface, 52px) holding items; the active item is a spotlight pill. At most seven items; otherwise use the rail.
- **Tabs and segmented control:** the same pill container, 40px items. They switch a view in place, never navigate away.
- **Breadcrumbs:** a screen opened from a list (the page of one user group, one cohort, one bank) has no back button. It shows the path above its title, in the admin top: `/ User groups / Group name`. Each step starts with a `/`, every step before the last is a link in the muted colour (light grey on Midnight; Body small, 600) that turns to the text colour (white on Midnight, near-black on Daylight) on hover and keyboard focus, with no underline, and the last is the current page in the text colour. The path sits 12px above the title. The path replaces the "back" link everywhere, at any depth, and wraps on a narrow screen (the current step is cut with an ellipsis). It is the `crumbs` prop of `AdminTop`.
- **Mobile:** the rail becomes a drawer, and the five primary destinations may also sit in a bottom bar of 64px.

### Cards and tiles

- **Card:** surface, 24px radius, 24px padding, no border, no shadow. A header row holds a title (Title) with an optional muted description beneath it and an action slot on the right.
- **Spotlight card:** inverted fill. One per screen. Text uses on-spotlight and on-spotlight-muted.
- **Accent card:** accent fill, solid white text (secondary lines distinguished by size and weight, not transparency). One per screen at most, and never together with a second large accent area.
- **Stat tile:** a label (Body small, muted), a numeral, and one line of context: a change badge or "of 125 students". A figure without context is not a stat. No decorative sparkline; a trend line appears only when it shows real data.
- **Tile (inside a card):** raised fill, 18px radius, 16px padding, no border and no shadow. Only for selectable or actionable items that sit in a grid or a short list (a time slot, an upcoming test). Text-only items are rows with hairlines, not tiles. A tile never contains another tile.

### Inputs and filters

- **Text field:** 44px, raised fill, 1px field-border outline, 14px radius, label above in Label type, helper or error below in Caption. Focus replaces the outline with the 2px accent ring. Error uses the danger colour, an icon and a sentence that says what to fix.
- **Search:** pill, leading icon.
- **Select and filter:** compact pill (36px) with the current value and a chevron ("Last 7 days"). Menus are 10px radius, float small.
- **Checkbox, radio, switch:** accent when on, field-border when off, 24px target inside a 44px hit area.
- **Date picker:** day cells are 40px circles. Selected is a spotlight circle, available is raised, full or unavailable is high with muted text and a strike-through for unavailable, today has an accent ring.

### Tables and leaderboards

- Header row: Body small, 600, muted, sentence case, sticky. Rows: 56px comfortable, 44px compact, hairline separators, hover raised.
- Numeric columns are right-aligned and tabular. Text columns left. The first two columns are the identity: rank, then the student (avatar, name and the roll number beneath).
- Sorting shows an arrow beside the active header. Sorted state is also announced to assistive technology.
- The signed-in student's row is highlighted with accent-soft, carries a "You" badge and sticks to the bottom edge of the scroll area when it is off screen.
- Ranks 1 to 3 are numerals in small tonal circles (accent-soft, raised, high), never spotlight fills, so the one-spotlight rule holds. No medals or emoji.
- Row actions sit in an icon button at the end of the row. Bulk actions appear in a floating spotlight bar.
- Below 900px a table becomes a list of rows (identity, primary figure, secondary line) with the rest behind the row.
- **Band table (admin list screens, `.ui-band-table` in the kit):** a table with no card around it. The header row is a quiet band in the raised tone, 44px high, with fully rounded ends (`md` radius) and Body small 600 muted labels. Rows are 64px with a 1px `line` hairline between them and none after the last. A hovered row turns raised, with rounded ends. The person is a 40px avatar with the name in Label type and the email under it in Caption muted; a status is a Badge with a word; the row action (Remove, Approve and Reject) sits in the last column. Numbers are tabular and right-aligned. It scrolls sideways inside its own wrapper below its minimum width and becomes a list of rows below 900px. The students table of a user group carries the identical look.

### Charts

- Bars: rounded top, 8 to 12px gap, `bar-muted` by default, one highlighted bar in spotlight, a tooltip in a small pill (`high` on Midnight, spotlight on Daylight) with a caret. The goal is a dashed hairline with a small green tag.
- Lines: 2px, smooth, round caps, a dot on the latest point only, area fill from the series colour at 18% to 0%.
- Dot matrices and heatmaps use circles (or 4px-rounded squares) in a five-step ramp of the accent, with a legend.
- Gauges and rings appear only for a real goal with a real percentage, with the value in text inside.
- Grid lines are dashed hairlines. Axis labels are Caption in subtle. Legends are a dot and a label under the chart.
- Series follow the data-series order. Where two series could be confused, the second uses a diagonal hatch pattern, never colour alone.
- Every chart has a title, a text equivalent of its main finding, and a table view for assistive technology.

### Badges, chips and avatars

- **Badge:** 24px pill, soft tint with matching text colour and an optional 6px dot or arrow icon. For status and change.
- **Chip:** 32px pill, raised fill; selected becomes accent-soft with accent-text. Filter chips can be removed with a trailing icon.
- **Avatar:** circle at 32, 40 and 48px; initials on a tone from the series palette when there is no photo; a 2px ring in the surface colour when stacked.

### Dialogs, menus and toasts

- **Dialog:** surface, 28px radius, 28px padding, scrim behind, title in Headline, actions right-aligned with the primary last. Use a dialog only when the task needs interruption or protected focus; otherwise use an inline panel or a page.
- **Toast:** raised fill, 14px radius, float medium, a leading icon in the status colour and a sentence; auto-dismiss in 5 seconds, longer for errors with an action.
- **Edit panel (a drawer that creates, edits or adds):** the slide panel from the right (480px, the full width below 600px) has a header in the platform blue: the accent fill with white title and description, the close button a darker circle of the accent (hover and press tones), and the top-left corner at the dialog radius. It is the same blue in Midnight and Daylight. Every drawer whose job is to change something (Create, Edit, Add roll numbers, the course, opportunity, bank, topic and import drawers, the profile section editor) uses it, with `tone="accent"` on `Drawer`. The wide `Sheet` of the cohort and bank library question forms has the same blue header. A drawer that only shows a detail (`DetailPanel`) keeps the plain header. The body and the footer stay on the surface; the footer holds Cancel and the one primary action, last.
- **Tooltip:** 10px radius, Caption, appears on hover and keyboard focus within 150ms.

### States

- **Loading:** skeletons shaped like the content in raised tone, shimmering at 1.4s. A centred spinner is only for a full-page first load.
- **Empty:** a small authored illustration, a Title line that says what is missing in the product's words, one line that explains why, and one primary action.
- **Error:** says what failed and how to recover, with a retry. Never a raw message.
- **Stale:** data that is older than the last expected sync shows a warning badge with the time of the last sync.
- **No data versus zero:** a platform that is not linked shows "Not linked" with a link action; a linked platform with no activity shows 0.

### Interaction and motion

- 150 to 220ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` (exponential ease-out), on transform, opacity and colour only.
- Motion conveys state: a change, feedback, a reveal. Charts (bars, dials, heatmaps, lines) enter once on first view: each mark takes `duration-enter` (560ms) on `ease-enter` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, a long soft landing), the marks start in a soft wave (the whole wave is over within about 400ms), and the figures and names follow the marks. Bars rise from their baseline, lines draw themselves, dial ticks light up in a sweep, heatmap squares appear in a diagonal wave. No bounce, no elastic easing, no looping decoration, and no choreography between cards.
- **One container transform: a card that opens.** On the student dashboard the overall score tile, the college rank tile and the problems solved card each open into a panel that fills the whole dashboard. The panel is shown through a window (`clip-path`) that starts exactly on the card and grows to the dashboard's edges over 540ms on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, rounded like a card all the way, so nothing is stretched. The panel fades in under the window, the cards around it fade out (240ms), and what is inside arrives a beat later; the charts inside draw after the window has nearly arrived. Closing runs it backwards in 400ms onto the card's current place, and the cards come back as it lands. Below 1000px the panel replaces the stack with a short fade and rise.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all movement and keeps instant state changes.
- Top bar controls (search pill, round buttons, account pill) fill with the spotlight colour on hover as a circle that grows from one point: the search icon, the avatar, or the middle of a round button. Text and icons turn to the spotlight's text colour as the circle reaches them and back as it withdraws. The search pill stays filled while it has focus, and the fill is its focus indicator.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** start every data screen with the figure the student came for, in Numeral L or XL, with its context beside it.
- **Do** use only the tokens. Colours come from the theme roles, radii from the scale, spacing from the 4px scale, type from the hierarchy.
- **Do** check both themes, all states (hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, empty) and the 600, 900 and 1440px widths before calling a screen done.
- **Do** use tabular numerals in every table, rank and score list.
- **Do** pair every colour signal with an icon or a word.
- **Do** give every icon-only control an accessible name and a tooltip.
- **Do** show when data was last synced wherever a figure comes from an external platform.
- **Do** keep staff and student screens in one visual language and change only density.
- **Do** format numbers and dates for the audience: Indian digit grouping for large numbers, day-month-year dates ("26 Oct 2026"), 12-hour time with am or pm, IST stated where it could be unclear.

### Don't:

- **Don't** put a "back" button or arrow on a screen opened from a list. Use the path in the admin top.
- **Don't** give an editing slide panel a plain header. Panels that create, edit or add have the blue header.
- **Don't** build an admin screen's heading, create button and search from scratch. Use `AdminTop`, so the title, the primary action and the search sit in the same place on every admin screen.
- **Don't** use backdrop blur, glass panels, glowing shadows, inner highlights or gradient fills on surfaces.
- **Don't** use gradient text. Emphasis comes from weight and size.
- **Don't** put a coloured border on one side of a card, list item or alert.
- **Don't** use more than one spotlight tile or one accent tile on a screen.
- **Don't** nest cards. A tile in a card is the limit, and a tile holds no tile.
- **Don't** put a tracked-out label or eyebrow above a heading.
- **Don't** use a second typeface, all-caps text, or a display face in labels and data.
- **Don't** use `#000000`, `#FFFFFF` or plain grey for surfaces and text; use the tinted roles.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows, neon glows, bounce or elastic motion.
- **Don't** use emoji or Unicode glyphs as icons, or mix icon families.
- **Don't** build a modal for something a page, a panel or an inline control can do.
- **Don't** let a decorative sparkline, ring or gauge stand in for data.
- **Don't** transition every property of every element; transition the properties that change.
- **Don't** reintroduce `#0088cc`, `Nekst`, `Inter`, `Instrument Serif`, `Outfit`, `Sora` or `Clash Display` anywhere in the interface.
